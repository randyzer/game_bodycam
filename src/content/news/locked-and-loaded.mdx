## What Locked & Loaded Changed

Locked & Loaded is the current major Bodycam update axis for this site. Steam News records the main v0.8 release on 2026-09-02 at 17:00 UTC, followed by v0.8 #1 on 2026-09-03 and v0.8 #2 on 2026-09-04.

The update matters because it is not a single balance tweak. Official update material and devlogs connect it to loadouts, attachments, Reissad Points, the tactical tablet, sound, Trenches, FPV drones, RC cars, quality-of-life work, and stability fixes.
