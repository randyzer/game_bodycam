## Practical Checks After Updating

1. Revisit controls if keybinds, tablet behavior, or mute options feel different.
2. Re-check graphics/display settings if film grain, frame generation, or visual stability changed your experience.
3. Re-test Deathmatch and Team Deathmatch expectations because official score limits changed.
4. Revisit loadouts if a weapon, light, magazine, or attachment looked wrong before the #2 fixes.
5. Treat Reissad Points numbers as current to the official note, but avoid turning them into a complete progression guide without more observation.
