## Loadouts After Locked & Loaded

Locked & Loaded made loadouts and customization central to Bodycam's current player journey. Official Steam and Reissad material describe deep weapon customization, attachment choices, equipment, and in-world access through the tactical tablet and Shooting Range HUB.

This page keeps the useful part public without pretending to have a verified database. You can plan a kit around role and tradeoff, but exact stat tables, formulas, rankings, and builder outputs stay out of scope until they are observed or sourced.

## Choose by Job, Not by Tier List

| Job | What to favor | Tradeoff to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Close-quarters entry | Handling, compact profile, fast target recovery | Less comfort in exposed long sightlines |
| Angle holding | Stability, visibility, controlled recoil feel | Slower reactions if overbuilt for precision |
| Support and scouting | Equipment awareness, communication, drone/RC context where available | You may give up direct dueling comfort |
| Learning matches | Simple, readable setup | Fewer specialized advantages |

## Attachment Discipline

Treat every attachment as a feel change until exact current values are verified. If a part makes a weapon look stronger but hurts your ability to track through body-camera motion, it may be worse for your actual play. Test changes one at a time and keep a build only if it helps the job you picked.

The v0.8 #2 patch notes also mention fixes to broken or incorrect weapon, attachment, equipment, magazine, light, and part meshes. That is a useful reminder: after a major update, visual presentation and behavior may change, so old screenshots or third-party stats need a fresh check.
