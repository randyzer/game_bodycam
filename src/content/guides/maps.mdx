## Maps Shape the Fight

Bodycam maps are not interchangeable arenas. Official sources describe photoreal tactical spaces, close-quarters buildings, forests, abandoned hospitals, trench networks, and at least one real-world CQB location reference. Your goal on each map is to understand what the space rewards before you chase a weapon build.

## Current Map Context

| Space type | What to learn first |
|---|---|
| Tight interiors | Sound, doorways, short angles, and reload discipline. |
| Open approaches | Exposure, long sightlines, and when not to sprint. |
| Mixed tactical sites | How objective modes change the safest route. |
| Zombies spaces | Team spacing, ammo conservation, and darkness management. |
| Trenches | The v0.8 update highlights a larger battlefield with trench networks, open areas, tunnels, ruined compounds, forests, and a central church. |

## How to Study a Map Without Callouts

1. Identify where fights become close-quarters.
2. Mark where you are exposed to long-range angles.
3. Listen for whether sound cues are clean, muffled, above, or below you.
4. Notice which routes are safe alone and which require a teammate.
5. Re-check after patches, because v0.8 reorganized the map list and put Trenches first.
