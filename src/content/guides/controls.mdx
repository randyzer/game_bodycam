## Controls Are Part of the Learning Curve

Bodycam strips away familiar FPS interface cues. Official store copy emphasizes no crosshair, no ammo counter, and no HUD, so controls are not only about memorizing inputs. They are how you recover information the screen will not keep showing for you.

Before changing anything, open the current settings and control menus in your own build. The v0.8 patch notes mention keybind clearing, key conflict handling, and tablet input fixes, which means older bind screenshots can become stale quickly.

## Control Priorities

| Control area | Why it matters | Safe setup advice |
|---|---|---|
| Aim and look | Body-camera motion can make over-fast aim feel unstable. | Start conservative, then adjust after live fights rather than from a menu alone. |
| Leaning and peeking | Angles decide many close fights. | Keep these inputs comfortable enough to use under pressure. |
| Reload and ammo checks | There is no persistent ammo counter. | Build the habit of checking before pushes and after messy fights. |
| Voice and audio awareness | Communication and sound cues carry tactical information. | Confirm your mic, mute behavior, and audio balance before team modes. |
| Tablet interaction | The tablet connects to loadout, settings, and match flow. | If an input feels swallowed or stuck, check patch notes and reset conflicting binds. |

## First-Match Control Routine

1. Clear or reset any inherited bind that conflicts with movement, tablet, or voice.
2. Test aim sensitivity in a low-pressure mode or practice space.
3. Confirm that interact, reload, crouch, lean, voice, and tablet controls are reachable without looking down.
4. Play two short rounds before making fine adjustments.
5. Change one thing at a time so you can tell what helped.
