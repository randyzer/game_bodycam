## Safe Settings Before You Chase Numbers

Bodycam settings advice needs a careful ceiling. The game is visually dense, update-sensitive, and hardware-dependent, so this page avoids exact universal presets, fake FPS gains, or copied sensitivity charts. Use it as a safe order of operations for tuning the Steam PC version.

Start with the [system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/) page. If your PC is below or near the official minimum, settings can help with stability and readability, but they cannot turn unsupported hardware into a recommended setup.

## Tuning Order

| Step | What to check | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resolution and display mode | Fix obvious scaling or ultrawide issues before judging performance. |
| 2 | Upscaling / frame generation options | v0.8 patch notes mention DLSS Multi Frame Generation, but benefit depends on supported hardware. |
| 3 | Heavy visual options | Lower expensive effects gradually if your frame time is unstable. |
| 4 | Film grain and motion clarity | v0.8 added a Film Grain setting; disable or reduce effects if they hurt target visibility. |
| 5 | Audio sliders | Hardpoint audio routing changed in v0.8 #2, so check UI/gameplay balance after updates. |
| 6 | Mouse sensitivity | Tune only after performance feels stable enough to aim consistently. |

## Practical FPS Boost Workflow

Do not change ten settings at once. Pick one test route, play a short repeatable scenario, and record whether the change improves frame pacing, target recognition, or input feel. If the change only makes the image worse without improving consistency, revert it.

Good first reductions are usually resolution scale/upscaling choice, shadows, heavy post-processing, and clarity effects that make the body-camera image harder to read. This page does not claim a fixed percentage boost because the official sources do not provide one.

## Ultrawide and Input Issues

Community discovery surfaced ultrawide and control-friction questions, but those are not official mechanics. Treat them as troubleshooting prompts: update the game, reset display mode, check aspect ratio behavior, then review keybind conflicts. If the issue persists, use official Reissad channels from [official links](/official-links/).

## What Not to Trust

Avoid any settings guide that promises guaranteed FPS gains, exact universal sensitivity, or hidden config values without showing current-build evidence. Bodycam changed around v0.8, and old advice can be technically valid in a menu but wrong for the current game feel.
