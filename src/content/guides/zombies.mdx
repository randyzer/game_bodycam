## What Zombies Is

Zombies is Bodycam's co-op survival side. Official Steam and website material describe round-based survival, dangerous environments, ammunition pressure, area unlocks, restoring power, multiple maps, and the darker found-footage feel that comes naturally from the body-camera perspective.

This guide keeps the safe public scope practical: how to approach Zombies as a player, what to prepare, and what not to assume from unsupported guides.

## Beginner Priorities

| Priority | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Ammo discipline | The mode leans into pressure, and the base game already removes the normal ammo counter. |
| Team spacing | Co-op survival punishes everyone clustering in one bad angle. |
| Route memory | Unlocking or moving through areas matters more than winning one isolated fight. |
| Audio awareness | Darkness and body-camera motion make sound cues especially important. |
| Simple loadouts | Avoid complex builds until you understand the survival loop. |

## How to Start

Enter Zombies with a teammate if possible, keep your first goal modest, and learn where your group loses control: ammo, darkness, routes, or panic reloads. After each attempt, change one variable. If you died because you could not see targets, visit [best settings](/guides/best-settings/). If you lost track of space, visit [maps](/guides/maps/). If your kit felt wrong, visit [loadouts and attachments](/guides/loadouts-and-attachments/).
