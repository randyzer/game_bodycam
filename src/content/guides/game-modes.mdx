## Pick the Mode That Fits Your Goal

Bodycam modes all share the same pressure points: body-camera movement, limited interface help, sound-driven awareness, and high consequence for bad angles. The difference is what kind of pressure you want to learn first.

| Mode | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wingman | Communication, clutch rounds, small-team pressure | Official Steam copy frames Wingman as 2v2 Search & Destroy-style competition. |
| Body Bomb | Coordinated objective play | Treat objective details as update-sensitive until checked in the current build. |
| Team Deathmatch | Learning fights with a team structure | v0.8 #1 increased the score limit from 50 to 75 kills. |
| Deathmatch | Pure weapon feel and awareness practice | v0.8 #1 increased the score limit from 30 to 40 kills. |
| Hardpoint | Position control and audio awareness | v0.8 #2 adjusted Hardpoint audio feedback. |
| Gun Game | Weapon adaptation | v0.8 #1 fixed an issue with too many pistols in the weapon pool. |
| Zombies | Co-op survival and horror pacing | Use the dedicated [Zombies guide](/guides/zombies/) for survival framing. |

## Learning Path

If you are new, start with modes that let you learn sightlines and sound without overloading yourself with team strategy. Then move into Wingman or Body Bomb when you can communicate cleanly and hold angles without fighting the camera.

Use [maps](/guides/maps/) when mode choice depends on space, [best settings](/guides/best-settings/) when fights feel unreadable, and [getting started](/guides/getting-started/) if the body-camera perspective still feels disorienting.

## Current-Fact Boundary

This page names official modes and a few sourced v0.8 patch changes. It does not invent matchmaking rules, hidden MMR, rotation odds, or complete scoring tables.
