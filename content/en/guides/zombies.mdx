The current [Bodycam Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2406770/Bodycam/) lists a round-based **co-op Zombies** mode. It describes players surviving infected waves across multiple maps, managing ammunition, exploring dangerous spaces, unlocking new areas and restoring power. That is enough to confirm the mode's broad shape, but not every map mechanic.

## The verified loop

The official description names several decisions that frame a Zombies session: survive each round, keep ammunition under control, open more of the environment and handle power restoration. The wording suggests increasing pressure across waves. It does not specify a universal sequence, the exact cost of opening an area or the number of waves needed for a particular objective.

A practical first-run mindset is to look for exits, share observations with teammates and avoid spending all ammunition before you understand the map. Those are broad survival suggestions rather than hidden game rules.

## Where competitive advice stops applying

Zombies is a co-op survival mode, while Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch and Wingman are competitive or team combat experiences on the store page. A loadout or route that suits one mode may be poor in another. The official materials used here do not establish a single best gun or perk for Zombies.

## Check the current build

Early Access changes may add or adjust maps, enemies and progression. When looking for a specific door, power switch or round objective, inspect the active in-game map and read current [Steam news](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2406770/allnews/) rather than relying on an old walkthrough.
