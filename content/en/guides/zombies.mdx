**For your next run:** identify a fallback route before the first wave, call out when ammunition runs low, and open new areas only when the team knows where to regroup. The [Bodycam Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2406770/Bodycam/) describes round-based co-op Zombies across multiple maps, with ammunition management, area unlocks and power restoration.

## Before the first wave

1. Look around the starting area and identify **two routes**: the path you intend to explore and a way back if the group is pressured.
2. Agree on a short callout for “regroup”, “low ammunition” and “opening an area”. Bodycam's official description says there is no conventional ammo counter, so practice checking ammunition state before a wave instead of waiting for an on-screen number. One player can make the next-route call while the others confirm they are ready; this is team organization, not a required game role.
3. Check what the current map actually shows about power and locked areas. Do not assume that a door, cost or switch shown in an older video still matches your build.

After a failed run, write down **one** cause: route blocked, team split, ammunition exhausted or objective unclear. In the next run, change one decision around that cause. This makes the guide useful even while exact map layouts change.
