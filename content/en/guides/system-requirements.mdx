These are published configurations, not a benchmark. Performance depends on resolution, graphics settings, drivers, thermals and later game updates. The developer's official site describes demanding visual effects, but it does not publish a universal “best settings” preset for every PC.

## A safe pre-install check

In Windows Settings, confirm the operating system and installed RAM. Compare the exact CPU and GPU models rather than only their brand families. Check the Steam page again immediately before purchase, particularly if your PC is near the minimum. If you are trying to solve stutter or low frame rate, change one setting at a time and compare the same scene; avoid treating an unverified community preset as a guaranteed fix.
