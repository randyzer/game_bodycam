**Quick answer:** save your current settings, choose one repeatable scene, change one option, and compare both clarity and frame pacing before keeping it. A universal “best settings” preset is not supported by the official material. Start by checking the <GuideLink slug="guides/system-requirements">Steam PC requirements</GuideLink> against your hardware.

## Make a baseline you can restore

1. In the Shooting Range HUB, use the tactical tablet's settings area. Record your display resolution, graphics preset, frame-rate target and any image-effect options shown in your installed build. A photo of your own menu is useful here.
2. Test the same spot at the range for about one minute. Note whether the problem is low average FPS, brief stutters, blurred visibility or input feel. Use the same resolution, location and camera movement for every comparison.
3. Change **one** option, retest, then keep or roll back the change. Do not combine a driver update, resolution change and preset change in the same test.
4. After an Early Access update, rerun the baseline; the same preset may behave differently.

The [official site](https://www.playbodycam.com/en-US) describes lens distortion, exposure shifts, motion blur and sensor noise as part of Bodycam's presentation; that does **not** prove each effect has its own toggle. The [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2406770/Bodycam/) confirms settings access from the tactical tablet in the Shooting Range HUB. [Patch v0.8 #6](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2406770/announcements/detail/705532193278002608) fixed a Film Grain setting that was not applying properly. If that setting behaves unexpectedly, check your installed version and later patch notes before assuming your preference was saved.

## If the first test fails

Restore the baseline and compare your CPU, GPU, RAM and storage with the published Steam requirements. If the game changed after an update, check <GuideLink slug="guides/updates">the dated update guide</GuideLink> and the current [Steam news](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2406770/allnews/) before chasing an older preset. A recommendation from another PC is a starting hypothesis, not a benchmark for yours.
