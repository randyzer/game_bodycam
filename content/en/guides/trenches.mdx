Reissad Studio presented Trenches as a new Bodycam battlefield in its [official Devlog #3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2406770/view/675126551624287628), posted 25 August 2026. The developer describes a large area that mixes close-quarters fights with longer sightlines, plus underground tunnels and several tactical routes.

## Map features named by the developer

The devlog lists trench networks, tunnels, dynamic weather and lighting. It also names ruined compounds, forests and a central church as points of interest. These details are useful for orienting yourself, but they are not an interactive map or a promise that every route plays identically in every mode.

The same announcement says there are dedicated areas designed for FPV drones and RC cars. It does **not** provide verified controls, unlock requirements or a complete list of modes where those devices appear. We therefore avoid a step-by-step drone guide until those details can be checked.

## Reading the environment

A map with both confined tunnels and longer exterior views can change how much information you have before an engagement. As a general tactical approach, name a nearby landmark when communicating, verify whether a route has an alternate exit and re-check visibility when weather changes. These are recommendations inferred from the developer's map description, not official instructions or tested optimal paths.
