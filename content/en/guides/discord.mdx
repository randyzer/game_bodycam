Use that developer-linked route or the Discord link on the current [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2406770/Bodycam/) to verify you are entering the intended community.

## Verify the destination

Check that the invite resolves to the Reissad Studio/Bodycam community before accepting. Discord invitations, server names and channel organization may change; this page does not claim a permanent invite, describe private channels or reproduce server rules.

## What to look for

A community server can be useful for finding other players and following developer notices, but a Discord message is not automatically a shipped patch. When you need a stable record of a game change, compare the statement with [official Steam news](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2406770/allnews/) and note its date.

If you are trying to queue with friends, be aware that [patch #6](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2406770/announcements/detail/705532193278002608) said the new party system was still under development on 25 September 2026. Check for a newer official announcement before relying on a social-media how-to.
