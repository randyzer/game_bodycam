On 6 August 2026, Reissad Studio [announced](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2406770/view/674001920444073955) **Bodycam v0.8: Locked & Loaded** for 2 September 2026. The developer called it a major overhaul rather than a small content drop. This article records what the announcement said and distinguishes it from later patch evidence.

## Announced areas of change

The developer described new gameplay systems, technology, animations, audio, maps and progression. Its four-part devlog plan separated the work into animation and loadouts, sound design, Trenches, and player-driven improvements. The announcement specifically named weapon customization and attachments in the animation/loadout topic. It did not provide a permanent tier list or precise best configuration.

The [Trenches devlog](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2406770/view/675126551624287628) later described that map's tunnels, weather, landmarks and varied engagement ranges. Our <GuideLink slug="guides/trenches">Trenches guide</GuideLink> uses only those documented details.

## Later patch context

Official [patch #6](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2406770/announcements/detail/705532193278002608), dated 25 September 2026, lists host-migration player-limit fixes, weapon reload and ammunition-visibility fixes, and a Film Grain setting correction. The patch also says the new party system was still in development at that date. That later note matters when interpreting old friend-queue instructions.

## What may have changed since

An announcement describes intended scope; a patch note records a specific later state. Neither guarantees that all details remain the same months later. Read the [official Steam news feed](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2406770/allnews/) for newer notes before applying version-sensitive advice.
